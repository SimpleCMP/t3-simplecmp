{namespace core=TYPO3\CMS\Core\ViewHelpers} Shared tab nav for the simplecmp_detections module. The two URIs `uri_detectionsTab` and `uri_servicesTab` are assigned by both controllers — we don't construct them in Fluid because Extbase's URI builder is scoped to the current controller and can't switch cleanly. Pass `active="detections"` or `active="services"` from the parent template to drive the active-state styling.
