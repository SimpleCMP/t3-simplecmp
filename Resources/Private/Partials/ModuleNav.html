Shared tab nav for the simplecmp_detections module. Group A — Konfiguration (Entwurf): tabs that operate on draft state. Group B — Betrieb: tabs that are independent of the draft workflow. The URIs are assigned by each controller — we don't construct them in Fluid because Extbase's URI builder is scoped to the current controller and can't switch cleanly. Pass `active="..."` from the parent template to drive the active-state styling.
