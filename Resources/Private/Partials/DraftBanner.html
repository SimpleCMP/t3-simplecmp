Phase-4 Draft-Workspace-Banner. Rendered at the top of every BE module tab. Three states: 1. No draft + no lock anywhere: "Live-Modus" hint (compact). 2. Draft owned by current user: "Du arbeitest im Entwurf" + Publish/Discard buttons. 3. Lock held by another user: conflict view + Takeover button.
