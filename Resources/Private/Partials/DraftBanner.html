Phase-4 Draft-Workspace-Banner. Rendered at the top of every BE module tab. Three states: 1. No draft: locked alert with "Create draft" button — ALL write actions gated. 2. Draft owned by current user: "Du arbeitest im Entwurf" + Publish/Discard buttons. 3. Lock held by another user: conflict view + Takeover button.
